Our Story

Helping students start every school day right

Shree Sawariya Ji – Uniform & Stationery is a neighbourhood shop located at Kalptaru Parisar, near AIMS Hospital and Omkar School Gate No. 6 in M.I.D.C., Dombivli (East).

Managed by Govind Singh, the shop specialises in school uniforms, books and stationery. Our aim is simple — make school shopping quick, easy and stress-free for parents, so children get everything they need on time.

  • Personal attention for every customer
  • Help in choosing the right size and the right items
  • Enquiries welcome on phone and WhatsApp
